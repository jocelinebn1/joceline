我对你而言就像风

昨天我遇见了你，我不喜欢昨天。 因为我知道，总有一天你会离开我。 我对你而言就像风。 我不能拥有你，你也不能拥有我。 你会尝试接触我，但你找不到我。 我会在这里照顾你。
English Translation

Yesterday I met you, I don't like yesterday. Because I know, one day you will leave me. I am to you like the wind. I cannot have you, and you cannot have me. You will try to reach me, but you cannot find me. I will be here looking after you.

Meaning

This poem reflects on a shifting friendship and emotional distance. Like the wind, one person's care and presence remain a constant, subtle breeze in their friend's life—felt and appreciated but holding no containment, demands, or close proximity. It is a gentle reminder that care can transcend distance and change.
